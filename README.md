## About the Game

**Tsunami Brainrot Heist** is an action-packed 3D Roblox survival heist experience:
- **Heist & Vault Mechanics**: Infiltrate high-tier brainrot vaults, crack security, and loot rare brainrot memes (Skibidi, Sigma, Rizzler, Mewing King, Grimace, etc.).
- **Tsunami Survival**: Giant procedural tidal waves sweep through the island arena every 45-60 seconds. Climb terrain, seek high-ground bunkers, and survive with your loot!
- **Tycoon & Safezone**: Bank your stolen brainrot wealth in your customizable base to purchase speed coils, gravity boots, and shield buffs.
- **Full Luau Architecture**: Built with Rojo, modular Luau server/client services, and connected via Roblox Studio MCP.
